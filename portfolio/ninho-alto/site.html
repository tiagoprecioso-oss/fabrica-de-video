Origin

Why altitude changes the cup

Cooler nights in the mountains slow the coffee cherry down. Slower ripening means denser beans and more sugar, which is where the fruit and the sweetness in your cup come from. Our lots sit between 1,100 and 1,400 meters, picked by hand when the cherries turn red or yellow.
