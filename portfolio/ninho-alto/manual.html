§ 01

Concept

What we sell, to whom, and the one promise every piece has to keep.

Promise

Grown above 1,100 m. Roasted the week you order.

Audience

US coffee drinkers who buy whole bean online, care about origin data and brew at home (pour-over, espresso).

Rule

Lead with the product and a real number. If a sentence or an image could belong to any coffee brand, cut it.

§ 02

Voice

Direct and technical without being snobbish. Every sentence carries one concrete fact: an altitude, a process, a number of hours.

Write like this
  • Picked at 1,400 m. Fermented 72 hours without oxygen. Tastes like red berries and panela.
  • Roasted Monday, at your door by Thursday.
  • Use 18 g of coffee for 300 g of water. Start at 93 °C.
Never like this
  • Elevate your morning ritual.
  • The finest beans on Earth, handpicked with love.
  • Exclamation marks, emoji, and superlatives without a number.
§ 03

Logo

The wordmark is the logo: NINHO ALTO in Hanken Grotesk SemiBold, capitals, tracked 0.32 em. It ships as outlined vector files, so it never depends on an installed font.

Clear space

Clear space on every side equals the cap height of the wordmark (H).

Minimum size

Screen: 96 px wide. Print: 20 mm wide. Below that, use the altitude mark.

96 px

Backgrounds

Ink wordmark on light grounds, Paper wordmark on dark grounds and lot colours. Over photos, only on a calm area of the slab or wall.

Altitude mark

For small square spaces only: avatar, favicon, stamp on the bag gusset. Four contour lines tightening up a slope, and the altitude dot, drawn by the same idea as the labels. It never replaces the wordmark on a page or a pack front.

Don’t

  • Do not set it in another typeface or weight.
  • Do not tighten the letter spacing.
  • Do not add the old sun-and-mountain symbol or any mascot.
  • Do not place it over a busy part of a photo.
  • Do not use it in lowercase.
  • Do not outline, shadow or gradient it.

§ 06

Contour labels

The one idea only this brand has: every label is a contour map of the slope where the lot was picked, with a dot on the peak and its altitude beside it. The maps are generated, so a new lot gets a new map from a new seed.

Lines22 contour levels, 2.6 px at label size, in the lot colour only.
DotInk, always on the highest point. The altitude sits to its right in Plex Mono.
BandThe bottom band is the lot colour with Paper text: weight and the word CONCEPT.
Generatortools/rotulo_ninho.py reads names, colours, altitudes and seeds from marca.json.
§ 07

Photography

All product images are 3D renders made in the factory, so the light, the slab and the bags are identical from shot to shot.

LightOne soft window light from camera left, about 45°. Shadows fall right and stay open.
SurfaceGrey stone slab (Slab tone). No wood, no burlap, no linen.
PropsBeans, one ceramic cup, a pour-over cone. Never more than two at once.
Lens50 mm equivalent, eye level or 30° above. No fisheye, no dutch angle.
CropProducts 4:5. Hero 16:9 on desktop, 4:3 on phones. Vertical video 9:16.
SourceEvery image is a Blender render from blender/ninho_produto.py. No stock photos.
§ 08

Motion

Video follows the same calm as the photos. The dot below moves with the brand’s camera easing over six seconds.

SpeedSlow and physical. Camera moves take 5–8 s; nothing snaps.
CameraDolly in, slow orbit up to 30°, or a locked shot. No zoom punches, no whip pans.
PhysicsBeans fall, bounce and settle at real-world speed under real gravity. No slow-motion tricks.
EasingEase-in-out (cubic) on every camera move; linear only for falling objects.
LengthReels and ads 8–15 s and they loop: the last frame matches the first.
TypeText cuts in and out, never flies. One line at a time, Body or Lead size, Label for data.
SoundRoom tone and the sound of beans. No stock music swells.
